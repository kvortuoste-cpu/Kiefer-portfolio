I'm Kiefer (key-fur), a Product Designer from Toronto, Canada. I believe design — alongside technology and research — has the capacity to improve business, society, and the human condition. Designing a good experience is not just a requirement, it's a deeper responsibility to the people we serve and the relationships we build.

In my free time, I'm a collector of hobbies but the ones I show most love to are making music, drinking bubble tea, watching anime, and going to the gym.

My Values
Life Learner

The pace of change is relentless. I believe growth comes from staying curious, absorbing new frameworks, tools, understanding emerging patterns, and questioning what I thought I knew. It's how I stay informed and relevant, while bringing fresh perspective to every project.

Design as Translation

Design happens in conversation. I work to surface insights across disciplines, ask questions, and create space for different expertise to strengthen the work. The best outcomes emerge when knowledge flows openly between designers, PMs, engineers, and stakeholders.

Be Human

Being human is to understand humans. In a world full of technology, it's my responsibility to empathize with people to make digital experiences more human. In a world of infinite features, it's my responsibility to design with empathy first, creating experiences that feel natural and human.

Balance Needs

At the end of the day, what we design impacts people and businesses. It's my responsibility as a designer to see that both ends are satisfied.

Technology as a Partner

AI and emerging tools are accelerators, not replacements. I leverage AI technology to expand what's possible: ideating faster, building prototypes, testing at scale, and automating repetitive tasks so I can focus on nuanced strategic work that requires human judgement. It's my responsibility to know what tasks to delegate and what to protect.
