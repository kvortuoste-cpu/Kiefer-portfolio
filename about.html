I'm Kiefer (key-fur), a Product Designer from Toronto, Canada. I believe design — alongside technology and research — has the capacity to improve business, society, and the human condition. Designing a good experience is not just a requirement, it's a deeper responsibility to the people we serve and the relationships we build.

In my free time, I'm a collector of hobbies but the ones I show most love to are making music, drinking bubble tea, watching anime, and going to the gym.

My Values

Life Learner

The world moves fast and there's so much to learn out there. It's my responsibility to stay informed and relevant.

Collaboration

Working to share knowledge, experience, and expertise — openly collaborating and communicating with team members.

Be Human

Being human is to understand humans. In a world full of technology, it's my responsibility to empathize with people.

Balance Needs

What we design impacts people and businesses. It's my responsibility as a designer to see that both ends are satisfied.
