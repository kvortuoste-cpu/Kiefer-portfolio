Regain confidence and control over your subscriptions

A subscription management app that enables users to track all their subscriptions, get billing reminders, and take back control of their spending.

The Problem

As subscriptions become a prominent business model for today's digital services, tracking and managing them has become increasingly frustrating. After initial research and a survey, three core problem areas were identified:

  • Identifying subscriptions — people don't know what they're subscribed to
  • Tracking subscription expenses — no clear view of total monthly spend
  • Subscription billing — missed renewals and unexpected charges

Research

A persona was created from the initial research and survey results, shaping the core theme of "regaining confidence and control over subscriptions." A competitive audit followed, analysing existing subscription management platforms in the market.

Personas for Subbox

Competitive audit

Competitors were found to limit key features behind a paywall, and subscription management was done manually — a tedious experience. Identifying these gaps helped translate each problem area into a functional feature:

  • Identifying subscriptions → View all subscriptions in one place
  • Tracking subscription expenses → View overall subscription expenses
  • Subscription billing → Billing reminders

The Solution

A subscription management app that enables users to confidently track subscriptions — viewing all their subscriptions, getting billing reminders, and editing subscriptions in one place.

User Flow

A user flow was initially mapped to let users input their email so all subscriptions associated with that email would automatically be pulled into the app. After consulting with a developer, the flow had to be revised due to technology limitations around email access permissions.

Reflection

This project helped me adopt a more user-centric approach, utilize a competitive audit to identify design gaps, and — crucially — collaborate alongside developers to achieve feasible solutions. The revision of the user flow after developer consultation was a key lesson in design feasibility: a great user experience must also be technically buildable.

Next steps would be to test the design with real users and iterate based on feedback, particularly around onboarding and the manual entry flow introduced after the email-based approach was revised.
